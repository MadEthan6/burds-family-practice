# Burds Family Practice: Ad Copy

Images are in `ads/out/` (each campaign has a square 1080×1080 feed image, a 1080×1920 story, and a 1200×628 landscape). Rebuild anytime with `python3 ads/build_ads.py`.
Link each ad to its matching page on burdsfamilypractice.com. Suggested targeting: 15-mile radius around Peosta (including Dubuque, Epworth, Farley, Dyersville, Asbury, and Cascade).

> Meta and Google health-ad rule: The copy cannot imply knowledge of personal medical conditions (no "Are you sick?", "Struggling with diabetes?"). All copy below is written to comply. Keep that in mind if editing.

Personalized health care in your community.

This is us.

Burds Family Practice is a welcoming community that empowers individuals and families to achieve their highest potential.

When you are feeling your best, you can do what you love to the fullest degree. We look forward to helping you and your entire family achieve vibrant health! Our mission is to restore and maintain the health of as many individuals as possible. We do this by addressing the root cause of your health concerns (not simply masking the symptoms). We provide personalized, safe, gentle, and effective care for newborn babies for even the most complex cases.

Note from Jenny.

“Healthcare has been my calling my whole life, and I am truly honored to be in a profession I love. With the opening of my medical clinic in Peosta, I can now do what I love in the city I love.

Peosta holds a special place for me as the Burds family were some of the founding members of Peosta. I am proud of this community and excited to be a part of its continued growth. I care about the people here, which is evident in my care for everyone who walks into one of my exam rooms.

Because community is so important to me, you will find elements of the local area throughout the office. From local builders who are working hard to make my space efficient and practical to resident artists displaying their artwork throughout to partnering with local vendors and providers, I’ve ensured that our community is well represented. I have carefully reflected on what our citizens need and have selected those medical services that benefit you most as a community.”

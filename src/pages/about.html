A Note From Jenny

Doing what I love, in the town I love

Healthcare has been Jenny Burds' calling her entire career. Opening an independent practice in Peosta let her care for neighbors in the place that means the most to her. The Burds family was among Peosta's founding families, and she is proud to be part of the town's growth.

Community runs through the entire clinic: local builders shaped our suite, local artists' work hangs on our walls, and we partner with local vendors and fellow small businesses whenever we can.

Rooted in Peosta

The compass, the leaves, and the roots

Our emblem pairs a compass star with leaves reaching upward and roots anchored deep below. That reflects how we practice: guiding families through health decisions while staying grounded in the Peosta community we grew up in.

We also invest outside our walls, serving as a Platinum Sponsor of the 'Be A Light' 5K Mental Health Awareness Fun Run and supporting local youth and wellness programs across western Dubuque County.

Our Team

Providers who know your name

You see the same trusted faces at every visit, backed by decades of family medicine, mental health, and clinic experience.

Jenny Burds, DNP, ARNP

Family Nurse Practitioner · Owner

With 30 years in healthcare, including more than 15 years as a nurse practitioner and a Doctorate from Clarke University, Jenny founded Burds Family Practice (Individual NPI 1639433030) to bring unhurried family care home to Peosta. She is also an FMCSA National Registry Certified Medical Examiner for DOT physicals. Outside the clinic, she loves workout classes and traveling.

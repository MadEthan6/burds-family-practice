Primary Care

Comprehensive care, close to home

Everything your family expects from a primary care home, right in Peosta Commercial Court.

Wellness exams & physicals

Annual physicals for every age, plus school, sports, camp, and scout physicals. Let us know which forms you need when booking so we have everything ready.

Same-day sick visits

Illnesses, fevers, ear infections, strep throat, minor injuries, and acute concerns. We keep same-day slots open so your family isn't stuck waiting days or sitting in an ER.

Chronic disease management

Long-term, collaborative care for diabetes, high blood pressure, cholesterol, thyroid disorders, asthma, and COPD, with treatment adjusted as your needs change.

Well-child visits & immunizations

Growth tracking, developmental milestones, routine childhood vaccines, and practical guidance for parents from birth through graduation.

Preventive care & screenings

Evidence-based cancer screenings, cardiovascular risk assessments, and preventive lab panels designed to catch concerns early.

In-office lab & procedures

On-site blood draws, rapid strep and flu testing, joint injections, and minor procedures, with all results explained clearly.

Referrals & care coordination

When specialty care or imaging is needed, we arrange the referral, communicate directly with the specialist, and follow up afterward.

DOT & workplace exams

FMCSA-certified CDL/DOT physicals, pre-employment exams, and drug screening. See Occupational Health.

Care That Listens First

Unhurried visits built around real conversations

Rather than rushing through a seven-minute checklist, we sit down, listen to the full story, and look for the root cause of symptoms. Many local families bring three generations to our clinic, giving us deeper insight into your family's health history.

Mental Health

Mental health care from people you already trust

Your primary care home is often the safest, simplest place to begin a mental health conversation. Our medical team screens for and manages mild-to-moderate anxiety, depression, and ADHD alongside your physical health.

For specialized psychiatric care, Jenna Hefel, DNP, PMHNP-BC is a board-certified psychiatric mental health nurse practitioner who has practiced psychiatry since 2015. She provides comprehensive evaluations and medication management for patients ages 12 and up, and holds a Perinatal Mental Health Certification.

When someone benefits from counseling or higher-level specialty care, we personally coordinate referrals with trusted local clinicians.

In crisis? Call or text 988 for the Suicide & Crisis Lifeline, or dial 911.

Common Questions

Good questions, real answers

Are you accepting new patients?

Yes. We welcome new patients of every age, from newborns to seniors. Call (563) 588-6659 to schedule your first visit.

What insurance do you accept?

We accept most major commercial insurance plans. Call our office to verify your specific plan, or explore our transparent Direct Primary Care membership starting at $99/month.

Can my kids and I share the same practice?

Absolutely. We care for newborns through older adults so your entire household can be seen under one roof.

Do you offer telehealth?

We are an in-person clinic first, with telehealth available for select visit types (such as certain mental health medication check-ins or DPC follow-ups) when clinically appropriate. Call to ask if your visit qualifies.

Do you complete school, camp, and sports physical forms?

Yes. Bring your required school, sports, scout, or camp paperwork and book early in the summer to beat the back-to-school rush.

Common questions

Coming to us from out of town

Do I have to live in Peosta to be a patient?

No. We welcome patients from Dubuque, Asbury, Epworth, Farley, Dyersville, Cascade and anywhere else. Call (563) 588-6659 to establish care.

Is there a family doctor near Dubuque accepting new patients?

Yes. Burds Family Practice in Peosta, just west of Dubuque on U.S. 20, is accepting new patients of all ages, from newborns to seniors.

Where can I get a DOT physical near Dubuque or Dyersville?

At our Peosta clinic. Jenny Burds, DNP, ARNP is a certified medical examiner on the FMCSA National Registry, and we offer same-week DOT/CDL physicals and drug screens.

Is Direct Primary Care available in Dubuque County?

Yes. We offer Direct Primary Care memberships for $99/month per adult, $79/month for a spouse or partner and $49/month per child, with unlimited visits and $0 copays.

Where do I park?

There's easy parking right outside our suite at 8411 Peosta Commercial Ct.

Ready to become a patient?

Call our Peosta office and our team will help you get started, wherever in Dubuque County you call home.

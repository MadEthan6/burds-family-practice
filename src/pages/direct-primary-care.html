Common Questions

DPC questions, answered

Do I still need health insurance?

DPC covers your everyday primary care, not hospital stays, surgeries, ER visits, or specialist imaging. Most members pair DPC with a high-deductible or catastrophic health plan (or health-share) so they're protected for major events while paying less overall.

How much does DPC cost?

Membership is $99/month for a single adult, $79/month for a spouse or partner, and $49/month per child ($276/month for a family of four). Call (563) 588-6659 to verify current enrollment options for your household.

Can I use my HSA or FSA?

In many cases, yes. Eligibility depends on your plan administrator and federal rules, so check with your HSA/FSA provider.

What if I need a specialist or hospitalization?

We refer you to trusted local specialists, transfer your history, and stay actively involved as your advocate and care coordinator.

Can kids and seniors join?

Yes. We care for patients from newborns through seniors, and families of every shape can enroll together.
